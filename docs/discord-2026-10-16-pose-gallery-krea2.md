**Pose gallery update: 113 new poses** (159 total)
The Pose Studio doll gallery for the Qwen Image 2.1 Pose Studio card now has a second collection: the Krea2 Pose Library.
https://michaeldune.github.io/simpligen-pose-gallery/

What's new:
- 113 poses: standing, sitting on the floor, kneeling, squatting, crawling, lying on the back or stomach, many of them shown from the front, side and back.
- Every pose comes as a square and a 3:4 portrait doll, with a slider comparing the doll to a real result.
- A new Collection filter at the top picks between General Poses and the Krea2 Pose Library, and the family filter now has a "lying" group.

What we saw: every one of the 226 new example pictures (a woman on the square dolls, a man on the 3:4 ones) came out on the floor in the doll's pose, including rear views, high kicks and crawls.

How to use it is the same as before: Picture 1 = your full-body photo (or a saved character), Picture 2 = a doll in the same shape as your photo. No prompt needed.

Credits: the poses come from u/Afraid_Ambassador_43's "120 Krea2 pose prompts" on r/comfyui (https://www.reddit.com/r/comfyui/comments/1v79la4/120_krea2_pose_prompts/), rebuilt as dolls with Pose Studio's pose-from-picture import.
