Good to know:
- The two Qwen Turbo cards share one 0.9 GB download on top of the pack. We also tested Qwen's full Turbo checkpoint (7.3 GB): same compositions, no visible quality gain, so the cards use the small LoRA.
- Turbo tends to give the same centred framing from seed to seed. For a different shot, change the prompt. And read every word before you use a picture: it wrote a recipe card perfectly and then dropped the accent from "Café".
- For editing, keep using Fast Edit. We compared it with the Turbo on nine edits and saw no difference.
- VXP Anima: for a known anime or game character, look up the exact tag on animadex.net first. Keep CFG around 3-4.
- WAI: keep prompts lean. The author warns that piling on quality tags or a long negative makes pictures blurrier, so the card adds only three quality tags. Rating tags work (general, sensitive, nsfw, explicit).
- Fast Drafts and every other existing card are unchanged.

Downloads: VXP Anima Turbo is 4.2 GB and WAI v17 is 6.9 GB, both from Civitai. Qwen cards are under the Qwen Research License, non-commercial use only; the other two follow their authors' terms on Civitai.
