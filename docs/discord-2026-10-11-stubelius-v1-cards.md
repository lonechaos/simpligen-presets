**Two new cards: LTX 2.5 Stubelius v1 Anime (T2V and I2V)** :underage: NSFW
The LTX 2.5 Stubelius Remix (Anime) pack gains two cards for Stubelius Remix v1, which came out of beta this week. Update the pack in the Store (1.1.0) and they are there.

What v1 is: Stubelius's beta 2 with one addition, OmniNFT, a fine-tune aimed at picture and sound quality. Same graph, same settings, same speed.

What we saw against the beta 2 cards, same prompts and seeds (3 text prompts and 1 image, 3 seeds each):
- **Text to video is sharper.** Every one of the 9 clips was visibly crisper on v1: faces, hair and backgrounds. There is also a little more movement, and colour comes out slightly less saturated.
- **Image to video looks the same.** We could not tell the two versions apart, so use whichever you have.
- **Dialogue and sound held up** on the clips we listened to.
- **Still NSFW only.** A clothed anime prompt came out with exposed skin on 3 of 3 seeds, the same habit as beta 2. Name the clothing and expect to re-roll, or use another model for SFW work.
- **Staging misses are unchanged.** In a two-person prompt the second character did not show up in the frames we checked on any of 3 seeds, on either version.

Good to know:
- v1 is its own 21.5 GB download. The encoder, VAEs and upscaler are the ones the pack already uses.
- About 85 to 100 seconds for 5 seconds at 480p on a 12 GB card.
- The distilled LoRA and OmniNFT are already inside the model, so do not add either as a LoRA.
- The beta 2 cards are unchanged and stay in the pack. If you only make text to video, v1 is the one to keep.
