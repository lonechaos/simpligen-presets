Good to know:
- Write the prompt as a caption of the finished picture, in full sentences: the kind of shot, the person with an adult age ("a woman in her twenties"), what they do, where, the light and the framing. No trigger word.
- Pictures are about 1.5 megapixels. 2:3 (1024x1536) is the author's recommended shape.
- Setting CFG to 1 is about twice as fast, and the negative prompt is then ignored.
- It uses the same text encoder and VAE as the Qwen Image 2.1 pack. If you have that pack, this one downloads only the 7.3 GB model.
- 12 GB of VRAM is the practical minimum, because of the text encoder. Needs SimpliGen engine 0.37.0 or newer.
- We have not tried LoRAs or editing on it yet.
