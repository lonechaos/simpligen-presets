**Four new cards: Qwen Image 2.1 Turbo and Turbo 4MP, VXP Anima Turbo V1, WAI Illustrious SDXL v17**
Three packs you may already have gain new cards. Update the pack in the Store and they are there.

The cards:
- **Qwen Image 2.1 Turbo** (Qwen Image 2.1 pack, 1.11.0): Qwen released an official 8-step Turbo this week, and this card runs it as a small LoRA on the model the pack already has. As quick as Fast Drafts, about 15 seconds a picture on a 12 GB card, and it fixes Fast Drafts' weak spot: a small hand-painted sign inside a street scene came out correct on 3 of 3 seeds with Turbo and on none with Fast Drafts.
- **Qwen Image 2.1 Turbo 4MP** (same pack): the same Turbo at Qwen's full 4-megapixel size. About 40 seconds a picture, against about a minute and a half for the 4MP card, with skin and small faces on a par with it. This is where Turbo looks best: at 1 MP it smooths skin and softens faces that are small in the frame, at 4 MP that detail is back.
- **VXP Anima Turbo V1** (Anima Anime pack, 1.3.0): VXP's Anima checkpoint with a strong, consistent anime look: clean lines, rich colour, solid faces. 14 steps, about 12 seconds a picture. Works with Danbooru tags, plain sentences, or both.
- **WAI Illustrious SDXL v17** (Illustrious Anime pack, 1.3.0): one of the most used Illustrious anime checkpoints. About 12 seconds a picture.
