**New pack: MiniMax H3 (DaSiWa Hybrid v4) - the fast one again**
Darksidewalker's v4 Turbo from 9 October is in the store. It is back to four steps, where v2 and v3 Turbo wanted eight. One checkpoint, three cards: **Text to Video**, **Image to Video**, **Reference to Video**.

Measured against the v3 pack on the same day (12 GB RTX 4070 Ti, 5 s at 480p, same prompts, seeds and references, Faster Attention on):
- **About 40% faster:** 42-54 s per shot against 63-81 s for v3. Through the app, all three cards came in at 49-51 s.
- Every identity held on three reference sets (solo, two people, person plus location) over three seeds.
- A scripted line came out word for word on three of three seeds.
- The look: more contrast and more background detail than v3, which keeps a darker, quieter frame.

Good to know:
- On one seed in three, a person-plus-location reference shot opened on the person's own photo for under two seconds, then cut into the scene. v3 never did this in our tests. If it happens to you, change the seed.
- The steps slider goes up to 8, but we only tested 4.
- Spectrum stays off, as on the other DaSiWa packs.
- v4 is its own 19.5 GB download and needs a Civitai API key (free model, sign-in required). The author also offers smaller 16.8 GB and 12.5 GB versions. This pack uses the full int8 file: the 12.5 GB one drifted more in our tests.
- v1, v2 and v3 stay as they are. v4 sits beside them.
