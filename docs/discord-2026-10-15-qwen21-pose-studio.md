**New card: Qwen Image 2.1 Pose Studio** + a free doll gallery
Put anyone into an exact pose. Give the card a 3D doll in the pose you want and a full-body photo of a person, and the person is moved into that pose while keeping their face, hair, clothes and the photo's background. It's in the Qwen Image 2.1 pack (update the pack in the Store).

**Get the dolls:** https://michaeldune.github.io/simpligen-pose-gallery/
46 poses (standing, sitting, kneeling, crouching, crawling, fighting, gymnastics, yoga), each in square and 3:4 portrait. Every doll opens with a slider comparing it with a real result. Download one and you're set.

How to use it:
1. A full-body photo of one person, with the floor visible down to the bottom edge.
2. A doll in the same shape as your photo (square or portrait).
3. Picture 1 = your photo (or pick a saved character), Picture 2 = the doll. No prompt needed.

What we saw (two people, four poses, two seeds, square and 3:4): every result stood, knelt or crawled on the floor in the doll's pose, 8 of 8 through the app and 32 of 32 in our ComfyUI tests. About a minute a picture on a 12 GB card.

Good to know:
- The person lands where the doll stands, at the doll's size. The gallery's dolls are all framed the same way so people land on the floor; dolls from elsewhere can make low poses float.
- A side-view doll sometimes comes out as a three-quarter view, and new seeds give nearly the same picture.
- The result takes the doll's size and shape; the aspect ratio setting is ignored.
- If you have the Qwen Image 2.1 pack, this card downloads only the 0.16 GB pose LoRA.
